Today, we’re excited to add Microsoft Teams as a notification channel in Socket. Security and engineering teams can now send organization alerts and supply chain attack campaign notifications directly to the Teams channels they already monitor, with filters that control exactly which events reach each destination. When Socket finds something that needs attention, teams shouldn’t have to keep another dashboard open to find out.
