Anthropic has launched OSS Scanner , a free, opt-in service that uses its strongest Claude models, including Mythos, to look for vulnerabilities in open source projects. Participating maintainers receive reports showing how to reproduce the vulnerabilities, along with proposed fixes when available, without waiting for Anthropic’s researchers to review the findings. The reviewers have struggled to keep up with the volume of reports.
