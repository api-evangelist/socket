Today, we’re excited to launch Socket for ClickUp in beta. On the heels of our Socket for Asana release , the new integration gives teams another way to send Socket alerts directly into the tools they already use to manage work. ClickUp brings tasks, docs, whiteboards, and chat into one highly customizable platform.
