This week our Threat Research team tracked an active supply chain attack that took over a maintainer account and used it to push malware across the widely used keyv and cacheable packages, then spread to other maintainers through stolen npm tokens. Those packages sit deep in dependency trees and account for tens of millions of weekly downloads. Attacks like this are getting more frequent, and open source maintainers are the ones on the receiving end.
