Socket identified 19 malicious extensions published in the last six months, delivering an extendable malware framework. Identified malware samples create WebSocket communication channel with command and control (C2) server, perform CSP stripping and abuse XSS injection to trigger execution of malicious payloads previously downloaded from the C2 server. Malicious capabilities are primarily focused on, but not limited to, wallet secret stealing and crypto draining.
