Cloudflare is acquiring Deno’s team, but the deal comes with an unwelcome surprise for developers who built on its JavaScript and TypeScript runtime: the company is winding down development, leaving the community to work out how to keep it going. Ryan Dahl, creator of both Node.js and Deno, announced on October 9 that the entire team is joining Cloudflare. Deno will receive another year of monthly bug fixes and security updates before the team ends its development work.
